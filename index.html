About Carol

I'm a therapeutic social worker with a Master's degree in Social Work, a Bachelor's degree in Journalism, a 200-hour trauma-informed yoga teacher, and a certified health coach, with almost 20 years of experience supporting children, adults, parents, and families across local government, charities, and private practice in London. I am registered with Social Work England and a member of the British Association of Social Workers (BASW).

My practice is integrative and inclusive, drawing on developmental and attachment theory, somatic and body-based interventions, and cross-cultural competence — shaped by training in Systemic Family Theory, Polyvagal Theory, and mindfulness-based approaches, and by lived experience across the United States, the UK, France, and Germany.

I work with individuals and families navigating identity, belonging, relationships, and life transitions, including specialist support for adoption-related therapeutic work and for those raising children across cultures, languages, and countries.

Work with me

Therapeutic, relational, and body-based services for individuals, parents, and families.

Mind Move Thrive offers integrative, evidence-informed support for adults and families navigating identity, relationships, parenting, and life transitions, including specialist support for adoption and cross-cultural family life.

Individual Wellbeing Services

Integrative, relational support for adults navigating stress, identity shifts, relationship patterns, grief, and life transitions — drawing on developmental and attachment theory, trauma-informed practice, and body-based awareness.

This support can be especially meaningful when life has pulled you away from what matters most, when old expectations no longer fit, or when you're holding both loss and possibility at once — relevant across life stages including adolescence, early adulthood, midlife, retirement, and reinvention.

  • Renewal and reinvention
  • Emotional regulation and self-trust
  • Home, belonging, and identity across cultures
  • Boundaries in relationships
  • Pressure and perfectionism
  • Loss and life adjustments
  • Reconnecting with your body, needs, and inner clarity

Parent & Family Consultations

Consultation for parents and families on attachment, regulation, behaviour, and family life, shaped by training in child development, Dyadic Developmental Practice, Theraplay, and life story work — including specialist support for adoptive families and cross-cultural parenting.

Yoga & Mindful Movement

For those who want to start with the body: trauma-informed yoga, mindful movement, and breathwork to support nervous system regulation, stress management, and a steadier relationship with the body.

Workshops, Speaking & Advisory

Workshops, talks, and consultancy for schools and organisations on emotional wellbeing, parenting, attachment, nervous system regulation, and cross-cultural life — available on request.
